How SmartQ Works

Built natively on the ServiceNow platform — no third-party infrastructure needed.

1

Browse & Join

Students check live queue depth and estimated wait from their phone before heading out.

2

Live Updates

Position and ETA refresh every 6 seconds via ServiceNow Server Script polling — no manual refresh needed.

3

Staff Calls Next

Staff click "Call Next" in the console. Flow Designer instantly emails the student their token is ready.

4

Feedback Loop

After service, students rate their experience. ACLs ensure data privacy — each student sees only their own records.
